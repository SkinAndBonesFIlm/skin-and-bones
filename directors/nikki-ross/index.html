ABOUT

Nikki Ross

Nikki is what one could call, prodigious. She has a gifted eye for capturing her subjects’ softer sides, and this brings an honest energy and lightheartedness to her work, one most directors only achieve after years of experience. With a solid understanding of portraiture, she has helmed campaigns for Nike, and GARAGE, and has been recognized by Applied Arts Magazine.
